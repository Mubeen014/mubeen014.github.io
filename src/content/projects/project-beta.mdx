## Problem

Placeholder copy. Thousands of semi-structured documents needed to become typed records, and
hand-written parsers broke on every new template.

## Approach

An extraction pipeline with schema-constrained LLM output, automatic validation, and a retry
queue for records that failed validation. Every run was versioned so outputs could be traced to
the prompt and model that produced them.

## Results

Placeholder numbers: 96% field-level accuracy on a labelled sample, and manual review time cut
from days to hours.

## Learnings

Validation and observability mattered more than prompt wording. Most failures were upstream data
problems, not model errors.
