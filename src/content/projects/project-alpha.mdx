## Problem

Placeholder copy. Event logs arrive at irregular times, and fixed-interval forecasting throws away
the timing information that matters most. The goal was to predict when the next event happens,
not just how many events fall in a bucket.

## Approach

A neural temporal point process with a transformer encoder over event history, trained by
maximising the log-likelihood of observed inter-event times. Baselines were a Hawkes process and
a binned forecasting model.

## Results

Placeholder numbers: a 14% lower negative log-likelihood than the Hawkes baseline, and calibrated
next-event intervals on held-out data.

## Learnings

Evaluation design took longer than modelling. Likelihood alone hides calibration problems, so
interval coverage became the main metric.
