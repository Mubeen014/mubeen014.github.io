## Problem

Placeholder copy. Each CFD simulation took hours, which made broad design-space exploration
impractical.

## Approach

A convolutional surrogate trained on a few hundred simulated geometries to predict pressure and
velocity fields, with an uncertainty estimate to flag designs that need a full simulation.

## Results

Placeholder numbers: predictions in under a second, within 5% of the solver on held-out
geometries.

## Learnings

The surrogate was only trusted once it said when it was unsure. Uncertainty estimates turned it
from a demo into a tool.
